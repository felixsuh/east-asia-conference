Workshop focus

How does connection shape resilience?

How do different forms of connectivity both challenge and sustain the resilience of the liberal script in East Asia? This workshop brings together scholars to consider connectivity as a constitutive condition for adaptation and contestation amid global change.

Across institutional, societal, and technological linkages, the program explores how liberal norms and systems respond to pressures without losing their capacity for transformation.
